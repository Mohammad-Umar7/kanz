## task
Edit the attached photo. It shows an everyday household item that its owner was about to throw away. Show the very same item after it has been upcycled by hand into this finished project:

{{after_visual}}

## identity
<!-- Image models like to "upgrade" the object into a new, perfect product. The reveal only works if the owner recognizes their own item, so identity comes first. -->
This is the same physical object, transformed, not a replacement or a nicer new one. Keep its identity: the exact shape, proportions and size in the frame, its color and material (glass keeps its tint and reflections, plastic its sheen, metal its finish, fabric its weave and wear, wood its grain), and distinctive details such as label remnants, scratches, dents or chips, unless the project itself removes, covers or paints over them. Change only what the project changes.

## scene
<!-- The app lays this image over the original photo in a before/after slider, so both must line up. -->
Keep the same surface, background, lighting direction, color temperature and shadows as the photo, and the same camera angle, distance and framing, so the before and after pictures line up exactly. Added supplies (paint, twine, wire, soil, fabric, a candle) look like real, inexpensive materials from home, applied neatly by hand but not factory-perfect. Leave any other objects in the photo exactly where and as they are.
